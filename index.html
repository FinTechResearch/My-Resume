THE ORIGIN

It started with a PDF that took too long to read.

The kind of chore everyone puts up with. A document too long to skim, text you just need copied out, and no reason it should take more than one click. So I built the click, taught myself the rest from whatever free tutorial was open in the next tab, and shipped it as a real tool, priced and listed on my own.

Then I noticed something worth noticing. That same extracted text could become an audiobook, if it passed through one more tool I had already built. Two small utilities, designed to talk to each other.

THE ITCH

The browser felt flat. So I made it move.

Music in one tab, coursework in another, most of the day. Everyone's browser looks like that. It always felt static. So I built FREQUENZ, a real time visualizer that turns whatever is playing into motion, live, in the new tab page.

Nobody asked for it. I just wanted the thing I stared at for eight hours a day to feel alive. When it turned out other people wanted that too, I priced it myself, in tiers, and shipped it.

THE ESCALATION

Then I built for someone else's time, not my own.

A task most creators know too well. Watching a video back just to write it up, scrubbing, pausing, transcribing by hand. So I built ScriptWave. Point it at any video, and it pulls the transcript, runs it through Llama 3.3, and hands back a structured, ready to record script.

Hook, body, CTA, teleprompter included. This is where it stopped being just for me.
